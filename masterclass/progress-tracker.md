## Reading your confidence
- **High confidence and wrong** is the most useful result you can get. It marks a belief to fix. Put it on the repair list first.
- **Low confidence and right** means you know more than you think. Review it once more, then move on.
- If your average confidence is much higher than your score for three days in a row, slow down and re-read the concept section before the next lab.
